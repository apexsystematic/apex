Cookie Policy

Last updated: May 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Apex Systematic uses cookies and similar technologies on this website. We operate a consent-first approach: analytics and non-essential cookies are only activated after you have given your explicit consent via our cookie banner, in line with GDPR and Spanish data protection law.

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites function correctly, remember your preferences, and provide information to site owners about how their site is used.

2. Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the website to operate and cannot be switched off. They do not require your consent.

  • Session cookies — temporary cookies that expire when you close your browser, used to maintain basic site functionality
  • CookieYes — our consent management platform sets cookies to remember your cookie preferences. Without this, we could not honour your choices. CookieYes Privacy Policy

3. Analytics Cookies

These cookies are only set after you have given your consent. They help us understand how visitors interact with our website so we can improve it. No personally identifiable information is stored.

  • Google Analytics 4 (GA4) — collects anonymised data on pages visited, session duration, and user behaviour. We operate GA4 under Google Consent Mode v2, which means no analytics data is collected until consent is granted. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Tag Manager — used to manage and deploy analytics and marketing tags. GTM itself does not set cookies, but it loads scripts (such as GA4) that may do so once you have consented.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some functionality on this website involves third-party services that may set their own cookies. We recommend reviewing their privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • CookieYes Privacy Policy (consent management)

5. Managing Your Consent

You can update your cookie preferences at any time by clicking the cookie settings link in the footer of this page. Withdrawing consent will prevent non-essential cookies from being set going forward, but will not remove cookies already placed during a previous session.

6. Managing Cookies in Your Browser

You can also control or delete cookies directly through your browser settings:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

7. Changes

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our tools or legal requirements. Any changes will be reflected on this page with a revised date.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about how we use cookies, please contact us.
