The model

Why this works

Most automation agencies build systems you depend on them to run. The retainer is the business model — not the build. We work the opposite way.

Every project is fixed price, agreed before we start. You own the system at handover — the code, the documentation, the credentials. There’s no monthly fee, no platform you’re tied into, no automation that quietly stops working when you stop paying.

If something needs changing six months from now, you can change it yourself, hand it to anyone, or ask us. Your call. The system is yours.
