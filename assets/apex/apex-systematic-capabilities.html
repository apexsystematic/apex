The Problem

Where the hours go

The admin is not a minor inconvenience.
It is a day of billable time, every week.

Research on professional services firms puts avoidable administrative time at around 8 hours per week per fee-earner. That is a full working day — spent on tasks that do not require a qualified professional to do them.

Intake calls that fall through because no one picked up. New clients chased for documents across three email threads. Invoices raised manually after every matter closes. Onboarding explained from scratch each time. Reports pulled together on a Friday afternoon.

None of it is complicated. All of it is time.

The most important finding in the research: the largest gains from automation consistently go to below-average performers. Bottom-quartile workers improved 43% versus 17% for top performers. Automation narrows the capability gap — it does not just help the best-resourced firms.
