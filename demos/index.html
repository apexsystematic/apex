AI Triage Engine

Lead Intake & Qualification

Evaluates every incoming enquiry against your criteria — matter type, budget, urgency — and routes it automatically. Qualified leads go straight to your team. Poor fits get a polite decline, sent for you.

Not every enquiry is worth pursuing, but someone still has to read them all and decide. This automates that triage. When a potential client submits an enquiry, the AI reads it, scores it against your criteria — budget, urgency, type of matter — and makes a call: qualified or not. If they're a fit, your team gets an immediate alert with a summary. If they're not, a polite response goes out automatically. Every lead gets a response. No time wasted on the wrong ones.
