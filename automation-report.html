Key Insight

The largest gains consistently go to below-average performers. AI is a floor-raiser, not a ceiling-raiser. Bottom-quartile workers improved 43% vs. 17% for top performers (BCG/Harvard, 2023). For solo and small firms competing against larger operations, this is the most significant finding in the literature.

The Stacking Effect — and the Gap

Workflows 01–03 compound: a solo professional with 15 client calls, 5 proposals, and 50+ emails per week can realistically reclaim 8–12 hours/week from these three alone.

But each tool in the stack above works in isolation by default. The gains in this report come from the connections — transcript to CRM, intake to calendar to invoice, draft trigger to approval to send. Building and maintaining those connections is where most firms stall, and where the real ROI either materialises or doesn't.
