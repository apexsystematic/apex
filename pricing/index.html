Intake Suite

From first enquiry to onboarded client — automated

The complete front-of-house sequence. A stranger becomes a qualified, onboarded client without manual intervention. Most firms start here — the ROI is immediate and visible. Fewer missed enquiries, faster onboarding, no manual back-and-forth.

#1

Enquiry & Lead Capture

New enquiries are captured and automatically scored — hot leads get an immediate, personalised reply; cold ones are queued. Each enquiry becomes a contact record with no manual input, with duplicate detection to keep your database clean. You get an alert when a high-priority lead comes in, so nothing slips through while you're in a meeting. Optional: a website chatbot handles out-of-hours enquiries and routes them the same way.

#2

Appointment Setting

Clients book directly into your calendar — no back-and-forth emails. Confirmation and reminder messages go out automatically (24h and 2h before). Preparation materials are sent based on the type of meeting booked. No-shows are flagged and a follow-up task is created; rescheduling is handled without your input.

#3

Client Onboarding

New clients fill in one form — everything else is automatic: their folder is created, your database is populated, and the engagement agreement is sent for signature. KYC documents are flagged for your review (not auto-processed — you stay in control). A three-email welcome sequence keeps the client informed through the first week. An onboarding tracker shows you exactly where each new client is in the process.

Client Suite

Keep active clients moving without the admin

Everything the client experiences after onboarding — requests handled, updates sent, invoices raised, documents and payments chased. Your clients feel looked after. You don't lift a finger.

#1

Client Intake Triage

When an existing client submits a request, it's automatically categorised and prioritised — urgent matters go straight to your inbox; routine ones join the queue. A conflict-of-interest check runs against your client database before the request is logged. The client gets an acknowledgement with an expected response time — automatically. You see all open requests in a single dashboard, ranked by urgency.

#2

Client Portal Updates

Every time a matter reaches a key milestone, the client gets a plain-English update — drafted automatically and sent without your involvement. Links to relevant documents are included in each update. The tone and level of detail are configurable by matter type. Cuts inbound "where are we up to?" calls significantly.

#3

Invoice Generation

When a matter is marked complete (or hits a billing milestone), an invoice is generated and emailed to the client with a payment link — no manual input. Disbursements and expenses are picked up automatically as line items. Your records update when payment is received. Connects directly to the Document & Payment Chasing automation if payment isn't received.

#4

Document & Payment Chasing

Outstanding items are checked daily — documents, signatures, payments. A four-stage chasing sequence runs automatically, with the tone escalating across days 1, 4, 7, and 10. The chase stops the moment a signature lands or payment clears — no awkward over-chasing. If nothing has moved by day 10, you get an alert and client folder access is flagged for review.

Admin Suite

Run active matters without lifting a finger

The internal engine. Documents drafted and gated for approval, deadlines tracked with evidence checks, call notes actioned automatically, data synced and reported on schedule. The work that fills your evenings — done before you've thought about it.

#1

Document & Proposal Generation

A first draft is generated automatically using the client's data — you approve it before anything is sent. The finished document is exported to PDF, saved in the client's folder, and delivered by email with a covering note. Triggered by a form, a status change, or an external event — whichever fits your process.

#2

Deadline & Compliance Tracking

Every deadline is monitored — reminders go to you and the client at 30, 14, 7, 3, and 1 days out. If required files are missing 24 hours before a deadline, an alert fires automatically. Missed deadlines trigger an escalation and an urgent task. A weekly digest gives you a single view of everything due in the coming days.

#3

Call Notes to CRM

As soon as a call ends, the transcript is processed — action items, decisions, and next steps are extracted and pushed to the client's record. Tasks are created with owners and due dates, so nothing from the call gets lost. A summary can be emailed to the client (you can toggle this on or off). Any mention of urgency or dissatisfaction is flagged automatically.

#4

Reporting & Data Sync

Pulls data from your CRM or project tracker on a schedule, reformats it, and drops it into a spreadsheet. A written commentary is generated automatically — plain English, comparing this period against the last. Delivered to your inbox with a link to the sheet. If the data looks wrong, you get an alert — not a silent push of bad numbers.

Retention Suite

Monetise relationships after the matter closes

The work most firms never get around to. Past clients re-engaged at the right moment, satisfaction measured after every matter, referrals tracked and credited. Running in the background without you thinking about it.

#1

Client Retention & Referrals

Clients who haven't heard from you in 60, 90, or 180 days are identified automatically. A personalised re-engagement email is drafted for each one — you review and send (it doesn't go without you). A review request is triggered automatically 14 days after a matter closes. Referrals from past clients are tracked and credited; top-tier clients get a referral nudge at the right moment.

#2

NPS & Satisfaction Survey

A short survey is sent automatically after each matter closes. High scorers (9–10) are prompted to refer — automatically. Low scorers (0–6) trigger an alert so you can follow up personally before the relationship breaks down. All scores feed into the client record — so you have a running view of satisfaction over time.
