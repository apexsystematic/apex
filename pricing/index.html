What to Expect

No hidden costs. No surprises.

What's not in the price

Platform subscriptions, third-party API costs, template and copy writing, and manual data entry to seed your initial Airtable base. These are running costs you pay directly to the providers — typically £50–£150/month depending on volume and stack.

What every build includes

Scoping call, full custom build, testing, handover, complete documentation, and a 30-day bug fix warranty. You get the logic, the code, and everything you need to run it without us.

How long it takes

Single automation: 1–3 weeks. Full suite: 2–4 weeks. Complete Firm: 6–10 weeks in sprints. Timeframes confirmed at scoping — never extended without your agreement.

Built to comply

ID checks and KYC steps are flagged for human review — never auto-processed. All automated sequences comply with GDPR opt-out standards.
