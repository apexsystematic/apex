AI Voice Agent

Voice Appointment Setter

An AI voice agent that automatically calls leads the moment they submit an enquiry form, qualifies them with a short conversation, and books a discovery call — without any human involvement.

When a potential client fills out your contact form, they're at peak interest — but most firms don't follow up for hours, or days. This automation calls them back within seconds, while they're still thinking about it. AI Website Agent

Chatbot Assistant

A live AI assistant embedded on your website that answers questions about your services, handles common objections, and silently monitors conversations for buying signals — automatically capturing leads into your CRM when intent is detected.

Most website visitors leave without ever making contact. This puts a knowledgeable AI assistant on your site that can answer questions at any hour — about your services, pricing, turnaround times, whatever they want to know. If someone starts showing genuine interest, the system recognises it and quietly logs them as a lead, notifying you on Slack and saving their details to a spreadsheet, without the visitor having to fill in a single form.

AI Document Automation

Proposal & Quote Generation

Fill in a client brief and the AI produces a complete, structured proposal document in seconds, then fires the downstream workflow — drafting client and admin emails and logging the record to Airtable.

Writing proposals takes time — pulling together the scope, timeline, pricing, and next steps for every new prospect. This automates the whole thing. AI Triage Engine

Lead Intake & Qualification

A lead qualification engine that evaluates incoming enquiries against configurable criteria — matter type, budget, urgency — and automatically routes them: notifying the team on qualified leads and sending a managed response to those that don't fit.

Not every enquiry is worth pursuing, but someone still has to read them all and decide. This automates that triage. When a potential client submits an enquiry, the AI reads it, scores it against your criteria — budget, urgency, type of matter — and makes a call: qualified or not. If they're a fit, your team gets an immediate alert with a summary. If they're not, a polite response goes out automatically. No leads fall through the cracks, and no time is wasted on the wrong ones.
